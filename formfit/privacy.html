FormFit

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

In short

  • Your photos, signatures and documents stay on your phone. FormFit never uploads them.
  • FormFit has no accounts and does not collect your name, email or phone number.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, which may use your device's advertising ID.
  • FormFit Pro is bought through Google Play. We never see your payment details.

Who we are

FormFit ("the app") is an Android app developed by Sujal Sharma ("we", "us"). It resizes photos and signatures to the sizes required by online application forms and turns scanned documents into PDF files. This policy explains what information the app uses and why.

Photos, signatures and documents

All images and PDFs you create are processed entirely on your device. They are never sent to us or to any server operated by us.

  • Files you create are stored in the app's private storage ("My files"). Uninstalling the app deletes them.
  • If you choose Save to Gallery or Save to phone, a copy is saved to your phone's Pictures folder or to a folder you pick.
  • If you choose Share, the file is passed to the app you select (for example WhatsApp or Google Drive), and that app's own privacy policy applies.

Camera and photo picker

The camera is used only when you tap to take a photo or scan a document. Photos chosen from your gallery are opened through Android's system photo picker, so the app only sees the photos you select. The app does not request access to your whole photo library.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of FormFit shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers,
  • approximate location derived from your IP address,
  • app interactions with ads, and diagnostic and crash information.

This data is handled by Google under its own policies: How Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google advertising. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and UK), the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in Android Settings → Google → Ads (on some phones: Settings → Privacy → Ads). FormFit Pro users see no ads.

Purchases (Google Play)

FormFit Pro is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Google Play. Google processes your payment (UPI, card, Play balance, etc.); we never receive your payment details. The app only checks with Google Play whether your Google account owns FormFit Pro, so it can unlock Pro and restore it on a new phone. See the Google Privacy Policy.

Data stored on your device

The app stores a few settings on your phone only, such as your theme (dark or light), your last custom size, and whether Pro is unlocked. These never leave your device.

Children

FormFit is intended for adults and older students filling in application forms. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Data deletion

Because FormFit has no accounts and keeps your files only on your device, you can delete everything by deleting files in My files or by uninstalling the app. Advertising data held by Google can be managed in your Google account and Android ad settings.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above.
